# SASO Certification for LED Lighting: Complete Guide for Saudi Arabia Importers

**SASO certification is mandatory for all LED lighting products imported into Saudi Arabia.** Without it, Saudi Customs will reject your shipment at the port. This guide explains the SASO certification process, SABER platform registration, required documents, and how to ensure smooth customs clearance for your lighting products.

## What is SASO Certification?

SASO stands for the Saudi Standards, Metrology and Quality Organization. It is the government body responsible for developing and enforcing product standards in Saudi Arabia. For LED lighting products, SASO certification confirms that your products meet the Saudi Technical Regulation for Lighting Products (aligned with IEC 60598 and IEC 62560 standards).

SASO certification involves two key steps:
1. **Product testing** at a SASO-accredited laboratory (IECEE CBTL)
2. **SABER platform registration** to obtain the Certificate of Conformity (CoC)

## The SABER Platform: Saudi Arabia's Online Certification System

SABER (Saudi Product Safety Programme) is the online platform where importers register products and obtain conformity certificates. Launched in 2019, SABER replaced the old SASO CoC paper-based system.

### Steps to Register on SABER:

1. Create an account on [saber.sa](https://saber.sa)
2. Submit product technical documentation
3. Upload test reports from accredited labs
4. Pay certification fees
5. Receive your Certificate of Conformity (CoC)

The CoC is linked to your shipment's commercial invoice and is verified by Saudi Customs at the port of entry.

## Required Documents for SASO Certification

To obtain SASO certification for LED lighting, you need:

- **Test reports** from an IECEE CBTL-accredited laboratory
- **Product datasheets** with full technical specifications
- **Product photos** (front, back, label)
- **Manufacturer's declaration of conformity**
- **CB Test Certificate** (if available, accelerates the process)
- **SASO Energy Efficiency registration** (for regulated lamp categories)

## Key Standards for LED Lighting

SASO references the following international standards for LED lighting products:

| Standard | Scope |
|----------|-------|
| IEC 60598-1 | General requirements for luminaires |
| IEC 60598-2-x | Particular requirements by luminaire type |
| IEC 62560 | Self-ballasted LED lamps for general lighting |
| IEC 62722-2-1 | LED luminaire performance |
| IEC 62471 | Photobiological safety |

## Costs and Timeline

- **Product testing**: $500-2,000 per product (varies by complexity)
- **SABER registration fee**: SAR 500-1,500 per product
- **Total timeline**: 3-6 weeks from test report submission to CoC issuance
- **CoC validity**: 1 year (renewable)

## Common Reasons for SASO Rejection

1. **Incomplete test reports** — missing test parameters or non-accredited lab
2. **Incorrect product classification** — wrong HS code or product category
3. **Missing energy efficiency label** — required for regulated lamp types
4. **Label non-compliance** — Arabic language label requirement not met
