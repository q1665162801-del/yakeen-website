# G-Mark Certification: GCC Conformity Marking for Lighting Products

**G-Mark certification allows LED lighting products to move freely across all 6 GCC countries (Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman) without additional national certifications.** This guide explains the G-Mark process, how it differs from SASO, and why it matters for lighting importers in the Middle East.

## What is G-Mark?

G-Mark is the GCC Conformity Marking, established by the Gulf Standardization Organization (GSO). It indicates that a product complies with the relevant GCC Technical Regulations. For lighting products, the applicable regulation is GSO 25 / BDW7023:2021 "Lamps and Luminaires."

The G-Mark is recognized by all GCC member states:
- Saudi Arabia (KSA)
- United Arab Emirates (UAE)
- Qatar
- Kuwait
- Bahrain
- Oman

## G-Mark vs SASO: What's the Difference?

| Aspect | G-Mark | SASO |
|--------|--------|------|
| Scope | All 6 GCC countries | Saudi Arabia only |
| Issuing body | GSO (GCC level) | SASO (Saudi level) |
| Registration | GSO GCC Notification System | SABER platform |
| Validity | 3 years | 1 year |
| Customs | Accepted in all GCC | Saudi Customs only |

**Key insight**: If you export to multiple GCC countries, G-Mark eliminates the need for separate national certifications. However, Saudi Arabia still requires SABER registration even for G-Mark certified products.

## G-Mark Certification Process

### Step 1: Product Testing
Submit samples to a GSO-Notified Body or IECEE CBTL-accredited laboratory. Tests must cover:
- Electrical safety (IEC 60598 series)
- EMC compliance
- Energy efficiency (where applicable)
- Photobiological safety (IEC 62471)

### Step 2: Technical File Preparation
Compile a technical file including:
- Test reports from accredited lab
- Product specifications and drawings
- Risk assessment
- User manual (English + Arabic)
- Labeling artwork

### Step 3: GSO Notification
Submit the technical file to the GSO GCC Notification System. The GSO reviews the file and, if compliant, issues the G-Mark certificate.

### Step 4: Product Labeling
Affix the G-Mark logo to the product, packaging, and documentation. The G-Mark must be:
- Visible, legible, and indelible
- Minimum 5mm in height
- Accompanied by the 4-digit identification number of the Notified Body

## Applicable GSO Standards for Lighting

- **GSO 25 / BDW7023**: Lamps and Luminaires — Safety Requirements
- **GSO 34**: General requirements for luminaires
- **GSO 9**: Energy labeling of lamps
- **GSO 150**: Expiry periods of products

## Timeline and Costs

- **Product testing**: 2-4 weeks
- **GSO review and notification**: 2-3 weeks
- **Total timeline**: 4-7 weeks
- **Estimated cost**: $2,000-5,000 per product (testing + notification fees)
- **Certificate validity**: 3 years

## Why G-Mark Matters for Your Business

1. **Market access**: One certification opens 6 markets
2. **Cost efficiency**: Avoid duplicate national certifications
3. **Faster customs**: GCC customs recognize G-Mark for expedited clearance
4. **Consumer trust**: G-Mark signals compliance with GCC safety standards

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

- **Using non-accredited labs**: Test reports from non-GSO-Notified Bodies will be rejected
- **Incomplete technical files**: Missing risk assessment or Arabic manual
- **Wrong G-Mark format**: The logo must follow GSO's exact specifications
- **Ignoring national requirements**: Saudi Arabia still requires SABER despite G-Mark
