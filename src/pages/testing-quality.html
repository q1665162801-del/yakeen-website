Verification

Performance parameters verified.

Every performance value on the datasheet is backed by a test report from an accredited laboratory, run to a named standard. The report for your product line ships in the submittal pack.

Luminous Flux & Colour

LM-79

Total luminous flux, efficacy, CCT, CRI and colour consistency across the product range, measured to LM-79. The report for each line is supplied in the submittal pack.

Intensity Distribution

LM-63

Beam angle, intensity distribution and UGR, issued as LM-63 (.ies) files where available, for DIALux, Relux and AGi32.

Thermal Performance

LM-80 / TM-21

LED lumen maintenance data from the component manufacturer, projected per TM-21 to the rated L70 life under fixture conditions.

Electrical Performance

IEC 60598

Input voltage range, power factor, THD, inrush current and dimming compatibility (DALI-2 / 0-10V / TRIAC), verified to IEC 60598 test methods.

Ingress Protection

IEC 60529

Dust and water ingress verified to IEC 60529 (IP5X/IP6X, IPX5/IPX6, IPX7) for the exterior-rated fixture ranges.

Luminous flux, efficacy, CCT, CRI and chromaticity measured to LM-79 methodology. Each report is checked against the specified values before a batch is released.

TESTED

Electrical safety and performance verified to IEC 60598 test methods. Formal safety certification (CE, SASO) is issued by the certification body.

TESTED

LED lumen maintenance data from component suppliers, projected per TM-21. The projection states the rated L70 life under fixture conditions.

CERTIFIED

CE marking with LVD (EN 60598) and EMC (EN 55015) test reports from EU-notified bodies. Valid for all EU/EEA markets and widely accepted in the Middle East.

CERTIFIED

SASO 2902 product registration (PCoC) and G-Mark GCC conformity certification for all standard product lines. Issued through SASO-accredited certification bodies. Valid for Saudi Arabia and all GCC markets.

CERTIFIED

RoHS & REACH

All products comply with EU RoHS 2 (restriction of hazardous substances) and REACH (chemical registration) requirements. Full substance declaration available on request.

CB SCHEME

CB test reports accepted by 54 member countries as the basis for national certification — one set of test results, multiple national marks.

Standards & Affiliations

Standards referenced.

Each verification references a published international standard; the report behind it is supplied with the project pack.

IES
Illuminating Engineering Society

Photometric claims follow IES LM-79 (photometric testing), LM-80 (LED lumen maintenance), LM-63 (IES file format) and TM-21 (lifetime projection) methodologies.

IEC
International Electrotechnical Commission

Safety and performance conform to IEC 60598 (luminaires) and IEC 62031 (LED modules). Test reports are issued by accredited laboratories; certification by the certification body.

CIE
International Commission on Illumination

Colour and photometry values reference CIE 13.3 (colour rendering) and CIE 191 (glare). UGR calculations follow CIE 190 / EN 12464-1.

IEC 60529
Ingress Protection

IP ratings are verified to IEC 60529 for all exterior-rated fixture ranges, with the test report supplied in the project pack.

SASO
Saudi Standards Organization

SASO 2902 energy-efficiency registration for the standard product range, with PCoC certificates issued through the SABER platform.

GSO
Gulf Standardization Organization

G-Mark certified products for all six GCC member states. G-Mark provides direct market access across Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, and Bahrain.
