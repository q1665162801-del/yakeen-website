Testing Program

Tests we commission.

Photometric, electrical, thermal, and mechanical verification, commissioned through accredited third-party laboratories in our partner lab network. Yakeen's own engineers run incoming QC, final inspection, and verify every test report against spec.

Integrating Sphere

2.0 m · Spectroradiometer

Total luminous flux, efficacy, CCT, CRI, and color consistency measurement across the full production range.

  • Range: 0.1 — 50,000 lm
  • Spectral range: 380 — 780 nm
  • Accuracy: ± 3% (flux)
  • IEC-compliant calibration standard

Goniophotometer

Type C · 2 m radius

Beam angle, intensity distribution, UGR, and IES file generation. Applied to new product lines and modified optics.

  • C-angle: 0 — 360°
  • Gamma angle: -90 — +90°
  • Step resolution: 1° / 5° selectable
  • Output: IES LM-63 (.ies)

Thermal Test Chamber

-20°C — +85°C

High-temperature operating life testing, cold start verification, and thermal cycling for exterior-rated fixtures.

  • Temperature range: -20 to +85°C
  • Humidity: 20 — 98% RH
  • Ramp rate: 3°C / min
  • Interior volume: 240 L

Electrical Test Bench

Programmable AC Source

Input voltage variation, power factor, THD, inrush current, and dimming protocol compatibility testing.

  • Voltage: 0 — 300 V AC
  • Frequency: 45 — 65 Hz
  • Power accuracy: ± 0.5%
  • DALI-2 / 0-10V / TRIAC test rig

IP Test Station

IP54 — IP67

Dust ingress and water jet testing per IEC 60529. Used for prototype verification and batch audit testing.

  • Dust chamber: IP5X / IP6X
  • Water jet: IPX5 / IPX6
  • Immersion: IPX7 (1m, 30 min)
  • Verification: post-test insulation resistance

Burn-in Racks

200-unit capacity

Every fixture runs for a minimum of 4 hours at rated voltage before packaging. Early failures caught before they leave the facility.

  • 200 units simultaneous capacity
  • 100% of production — every unit powered on
  • Monitored: current, temperature, on/off cycling
  • Fault log per serial number

Standards

What we test to.

We test to established industry standards and methodologies. Some are third-party certified; others are verification tests commissioned through accredited partner laboratories. We label them clearly — no stretching the truth.

Honesty principle: "Third-party certified" means a certification body has tested and issued a certificate. "Tested to X standard" means the test is performed by an accredited third-party or partner laboratory using that methodology. The difference matters — and we want you to know exactly which is which.

LAB VERIFIED

LM-79 Photometric Measurement

Luminous flux, efficacy, CCT, CRI, and chromaticity measured by accredited third-party laboratories using integrating sphere systems, following LM-79 methodology. We verify every report against spec — it's our check that each batch performs as specified.

LAB VERIFIED

IES LM-63 Photometric Files

IES files generated from goniophotometer measurements following the LM-63 standard format. Files include full intensity distribution and are compatible with DIALux, Relux, and AGi32 lighting design software.

LAB VERIFIED

IEC 60598 Performance Verification

Electrical safety and performance parameters verified through accredited third-party laboratories following IEC 60598 test methods. Formal safety certification (CE, SASO, etc.) is issued by those same accredited bodies.

LAB VERIFIED

LM-80 / TM-21 Lifetime Projection

LED source lumen maintenance data from our component providers, calculated per TM-21 methodology. We commission thermal chamber verification through partner labs to confirm the rated L70 life applies under actual fixture conditions.

CERTIFIED

CE — Low Voltage & EMC

CE marking with LVD (EN 60598) and EMC (EN 55015) test reports from EU-notified bodies. Valid for all EU/EEA markets and widely accepted in the Middle East.

CERTIFIED

SASO 2902 & G-Mark

SASO 2902 product registration (PCoC) and G-Mark GCC conformity certification for all standard product lines. Issued through SASO-accredited certification bodies. Valid for Saudi Arabia and all GCC markets.

CERTIFIED

RoHS & REACH

All products comply with EU RoHS 2 (restriction of hazardous substances) and REACH (chemical registration) requirements. Full substance declaration available on request.

ACCREDITED LABS

IECEE CB Test Reports

Testing is performed by IECEE-accredited laboratories; CB test reports are accepted by 54 member countries as the basis for national certification — one set of test results, multiple national marks.

Standards & Affiliations

We test to established industry standards.

Our testing program follows published standards from international organizations. We're members of industry associations and contribute to lighting specification discourse.

IES
Illuminating Engineering Society

The tests we commission follow IES LM-79 (photometric testing), LM-80 (LED lumen maintenance), LM-63 (IES file format), and TM-21 (lifetime projection) methodologies.

IEC
International Electrotechnical Commission

All our products are tested to IEC 60598 (luminaires safety) and IEC 62031 (LED modules safety) standards. Third-party certified by accredited labs.

CIE
International Commission on Illumination

Our color and photometry work follows CIE 13.3 (color rendering) and CIE 191 (unified glare rating) standards. UGR calculations follow CIE 190 / EN 12464-1.

ISO 9001
Quality Management System

Our partner manufacturing facilities operate under ISO 9001:2015 quality management systems. We audit each facility annually to ensure consistent quality output.

SASO
Saudi Standards Organization

SASO 2902 energy efficiency registered products. We maintain active PCoC certificates for all standard product lines through the SABER platform.

GSO
Gulf Standardization Organization

G-Mark certified products for all six GCC member states. G-Mark provides direct market access across Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, and Bahrain.

Honesty note: "Tested to X standard" means the test is performed through an accredited third-party or partner laboratory following that standard's methodology. "Certified" means a third-party accredited body has issued a certificate. We label everything clearly — no stretching.
