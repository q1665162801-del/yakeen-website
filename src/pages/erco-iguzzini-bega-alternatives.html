FAQ

Questions we get all the time.

Honest answers to the questions every specifier asks when considering an alternative to premium European lighting brands.

Because we don't spend money on the things that make premium brands expensive — global showrooms, magazine advertising, multi-layer distribution, and large corporate headquarters. We invest in what matters: components, engineering, and quality control. Specification-grade components (Cree/Lumileds LEDs, Mean Well drivers), rigorous engineering standards, and project-based warranty coverage — at exceptional value because we sell direct from our manufacturing partners.

What components do you actually use?

We source from the same tier-1 component classes used across premium specification-grade lighting. LEDs from Cree (Cree CXB / XLamp), Lumileds (LUXEON family), and Seoul Semiconductor. Drivers from Mean Well (the world's leading LED driver brand), Philips Xitanium, and Tridonic. Optics from precision injection-molded acrylic and glass. Housings from 6063 aircraft-grade aluminum. We're fully transparent about component selection and provide datasheets on request.

Is the beam quality really as good as ERCO?

For the vast majority of commercial applications — retail, hospitality, office, residential — our beam quality meets the same specification requirements. We use tier-1 LED sources and precision optical designs, producing clean beams with smooth falloff and no visible striations or color separation. Where a project demands absolute optical precision, we'll tell you honestly and show you the photometric data rather than overstate the case. The best way to judge is to compare samples side-by-side — we're happy to send them.

How do I know the color will match?

We use 3-step MacAdam ellipse (SDCM) LED binning as standard — the specification-grade norm for architectural work. This ensures color consistency from fixture to fixture within a project. For projects requiring absolute color uniformity, we offer 2-step binning and project-specific color matching. We also provide complete LM-80 test reports, and color performance is verified in an accredited third-party photometric laboratory before your order ships.

What about warranty and support?

Warranty is specified per project — typically 5 years, with extensions up to 8 years depending on product family and application. Terms are confirmed in writing with the quotation. L70 rated at 50,000+ hours based on LM-80 LED source data and TM-21 projections at rated operating temperature. For support, you work directly with our engineering team — no call centers, no regional reps, just direct access to the people who design and build the products. Technical questions are answered directly by our engineering team.

Can I get samples before ordering?

Absolutely. We encourage it. We'll send working samples of any fixture you're considering so you can evaluate build quality, light output, beam pattern, and finish for yourself. We charge a refundable sample deposit that's credited back on your first production order. Compare us side-by-side with any premium brand — we're confident in the results.

Do you provide DIALux and photometric files?

Yes. Main product lines come with IES photometric files verified through accredited third-party and partner labs. For projects, we provide full DIALux calculation support — luminaire placement, light level calculations, false color renderings, and complete luminaire schedules. Our team can work directly with your lighting designer to ensure accurate calculations and proper fixture selection.

What certifications do you have?

We provide full international certification for all our products: CE (EU), RoHS, SASO 2902 (Saudi Arabia), G-Mark (GCC), and MOIAT/ECAS (UAE), built on CB test reports from IECEE-accredited laboratories. Certifications cover the complete assembled fixture, not just individual components. We handle all product-side certification documentation for smooth customs clearance across the GCC.
