The Italian Indoor Lighting Benchmark

Why specifiers compare Targetti with Yakeen.

Targetti Sankey is a cornerstone of Italian architectural lighting, founded in 1956 in Florence. Their indoor recessed, track, and linear lighting lines have become specification staples for commercial offices, luxury retail, and high-end hospitality across Europe. But for GCC projects — Riyadh, Dubai, Jeddah, Doha — specifiers increasingly compare them with Yakeen for practical, project-driven reasons.

Four reasons specifiers choose Yakeen over Targetti for GCC

1. Value on large indoor BOMs. Targetti's pricing reflects 68 years of R&D, Italian manufacturing overhead, and global brand positioning. A single office tower in Riyadh or a retail mall expansion across the UAE can call for 3,000+ indoor fixtures — the Italian premium compounds across every line item. We deliver specification-class quality — tier-1 LEDs, precision optics, aluminum housings — at a project-friendly price point.

2. Faster delivery to the GCC. Targetti ships from Italy. Lead times of 10-14 weeks for custom finishes and modified cutouts are standard. For fast-track office fit-outs, luxury retail openings, or hospitality projects across Saudi Arabia and the Emirates, that timeline is a blocker. We typically deliver 3-6 weeks standard, direct to Jebel Ali, Jeddah, or Dammam.

3. GCC climate-rated as standard. Targetti's standard catalog rates fixtures to 40°C or 45°C ambient, typical for European indoor environments. In the GCC, where office ceilings reach +55°C in uncooled service corridors and façade-adjacent spaces, that rating falls short. Every Yakeen fixture is rated to +55°C ambient — tested and documented, with thermal simulation data available on request.

4. GCC certification as standard. Targetti ships with CE and CB. SASO 2902, G-Mark, and MOIAT approvals for Saudi and UAE projects typically require an additional certification step, external agents, and 4-6 weeks of lead time. We provide full GCC certification with every order — SASO, G-Mark, MOIAT/ECAS. No customs hold-ups, no surprise fees, no delays at Dubai Municipality or SASO.
