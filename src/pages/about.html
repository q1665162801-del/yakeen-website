Quality Control

Five gates, every order

Nothing ships on trust. Each gate is documented, and the record reaches you before the container is sealed.

01

Incoming Material Check

LED chips, drivers, housings and optics are verified against the specification before production starts: brand, model, colour temperature, CRI and material grade.

02

In-Process Monitoring

Quality checks run at the three stages that matter — after soldering, before sealing, and at first-article approval. Issues get caught at the bench, not at the port.

03

Pre-Shipment Review

Electrical safety evidence to IEC 60598, photometric reports checked against the photometric data where it exists, colour consistency across the batch, and visual inspection under controlled light.

04

Thermal & Lifetime Data

Where the LED component provider supplies LM-80 data and a TM-21 projection, those are passed on with the batch, alongside the driver and thermal documentation held in the submittal pack.

05

Packaging & Final Review

Labelling, accessories, drop-rated packaging and a photo record of every batch. If it isn't something we'd accept on site, it doesn't leave.

On larger projects: the photo record from each gate arrives as it happens — not as a summary after the fact.

Our Network

Our engineering & production network

Production runs on five specialist lines, each working within its own discipline. Every run is inspected against our schedule before release.

Focused exclusively on recessed downlights and ceiling fixtures. Advanced CNC, deep-drawing and assembly lines with automated testing.

CE / RoHS / CB certified

Track & Spotlight Line

Specialist in track lighting systems and precision spotlights. Clean-room optics assembly, COB reflector development and 48V magnetic track expertise.

CRI 95+ capable

Linear Systems Engineering

Dedicated to LED linear lighting, profile extrusion and custom length fabrication. Anodizing, powder coating and specialized end-machining.

Custom lengths up to 6m

Exterior & Facade Lighting

IP66+ rated outdoor and facade lighting. Specialised in DMX, RGBW and extreme temperature testing (−30°C to +60°C).

IP66 / IP67 capable

Custom & Decorative Workshop

Artisan workshop for custom chandeliers, decorative fixtures and sculptural pieces. Hand-blown glass, metalwork and custom finishes.

Full custom capability
