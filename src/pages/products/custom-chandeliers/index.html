Structural Engineering

Before it looks right, it has to hang safely.

A 300 kg piece over a double-height foyer is a structural item, not a lighting accessory. We issue the load data your structural engineer needs — and we say plainly what is not ours to sign.

Document we issueWhat's in itWhen
Suspension load schedule Total weight, weight per suspension point, centre of gravity, and a dynamic factor for wind-sway in atria. With shop drawings
Anchor schedule Anchor type and size (chemical or mechanical), embedment depth, and the substrate assumption each one relies on. With shop drawings
Ceiling interface drawing Void depth, canopy and trim detail, access panel positions, and clearance to ducting, sprinklers and cable tray. Before ceiling closes
Electrical schedule Circuit allocation, total connected load, inrush current, driver location, and the dimming protocol. Before first fix
Installation method statement Lifting sequence, levelling procedure, torque values, and commissioning steps your contractor follows. Before delivery
Service & spares guide How to reach the LED and driver without dismantling the piece, part numbers, and recommended spares. At handover

Anchor and substrate

We design the anchor against the slab, beam or secondary frame shown on your structural drawings. If the structure can't carry the piece, we say so before production — not after it arrives on site.

Wind, sway and seismic

Tall foyers and atria move. Heavy pieces get anti-sway restraint and a lateral connection that is not tied rigidly to the facade, so the chandelier doesn't fight the building.

Serviceability at height

Every design has an access route for lamp and driver replacement — hinged arms, quick connectors, or a lifting frame. Above roughly 150 kg we plan a hoist with your contractor rather than manual lifting.

Power and control

Dedicated circuit, drivers mounted in a cool accessible position rather than inside the canopy, and a control protocol that matches the rest of the villa — DALI-2, 0-10V or Casambi.

Where our responsibility ends. We supply loads, anchor design and installation method statements. Structural adequacy of the building — slab capacity, beam sizing, the engineering stamp — belongs to your project structural engineer. We would rather draw that line clearly than pretend we can sign it.
Materials & Finishes

Specified, not decorated. Here's what's actually in it.

A custom chandelier is an investment, and the specification should read like one. This is the material logic we work to — and the substitutions we refuse.

01 — Metal

Brass, stainless, aluminium

  • Solid brass — machined or formed, not plated zamak
  • Stainless 304 / 316 — 316 for coastal and high-chloride sites
  • Aluminium 6063 — where long spans make weight the constraint
  • Steel — internal structure primed and powder-coated, never left bare
02 — Finish

PVD, plating, powder, patina

  • PVD — gold, black, champagne; hardest wearing, best on touch points
  • Electroplated — brass, nickel, chrome; traditional, needs care
  • Powder coat — any RAL, 60–80 μm, best for exterior bodies
  • Hand patina — antique brass and bronze, sealed with clear lacquer
03 — Glass & crystal

What the light passes through

  • K9 crystal — machine-cut, consistent, cost-efficient
  • Lead crystal — hand-cut, higher refractive index, heavier
  • Borosilicate — hand-blown, thermally stable, optically clear
  • Art glass / opal — colour and diffusion where sparkle isn't wanted
  • Acrylic — UV-stabilised grades only; we'll flag where it will yellow
04 — Light source

LED, driver, control

  • CRI 95+, R9 above 50 as standard, 80+ available on request where skin and fabric matter
  • MacAdam ≤ 3 step — no visible bin shift across one piece
  • 2700K / 3000K / 3500K, tunable white on request
  • L80 50,000 h, flicker under 5% at dimmed levels
  • Remote drivers — replaceable without dismantling the fixture
GCC duty

What 50 °C, salt and dust actually do

Heat

Air inside a lit canopy runs far above room temperature. We derate the LED current and keep drivers out of the heat — otherwise the piece is bright for two years and dim for ten.

Salt

Coastal villas in Oman, Jeddah and the Gulf islands sit in C4–C5 corrosion categories. That means 316 stainless and PVD — not chrome plate.

Dust

Open crystal arrays and fabric diffusers trap dust that nobody cleans at six metres. We design an access route, or specify a closed diffuser.

UV

West-facing glazing bleaches coatings and yellows acrylic. We specify UV-stable finishes and tell you where the risk sits.

What we won't use. Zamak and pot metal dressed up as brass. Chrome plate on exterior pieces. Unbranded drivers with no data sheet. If a budget forces one of those, we'll say it out loud and put the trade-off in writing rather than quietly downgrade and let you find out in year three.

Certification scope — read this before you write the spec

Applies

IEC 60598-1 and the relevant part 2 for electrical safety, plus SASO / G-Mark registration for the safety file. Structural load documentation for the building.

Does not apply

SASO 2902 energy-efficiency labelling. It covers standard luminaires by wattage and lumen output; a one-off decorative piece sits outside that scheme. If a consultant asks for 2902 on a chandelier, they're applying the wrong standard — we'll put that in writing so your submittal survives review.

Delivery & Responsibility

It arrives whole. Or we fix it.

Custom pieces fail in transit more often than they fail in service. The difference is packing, documentation, and who answers when something is broken.

Packing

Purpose-built wooden crate, every glass and crystal element in its own cell, desiccant inside, shock and tilt indicators outside. Not a carton with bubble wrap.

Documents

Packing list, certificate file, test reports, and a photo record of the piece before it was crated. Complete on our side — one set, not a chase.

Damage

Photograph the crate before opening. Report breakage within 14 days of delivery with photos; replacement elements ship by air at our cost. We keep the moulds and the glass recipe for your piece.

On site

Store crated, off the floor, in a dry room until the ceiling is finished. Installation is by your contractor against our method statement; we support remotely and can quote a supervisory visit.

Lead time for a bespoke piece is confirmed at quotation, counted from approved shop drawings, plus freight. If the chandelier sits on the critical path for handover, tell us at the brief — we sequence it first, not last.
