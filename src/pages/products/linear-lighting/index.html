Engineering Deep Dive

Why linear output tops out at 48W/m — and why that's actually good.

Spec sheets love to quote "up to 60W/m" or higher. But there's a reason we cap our standard linear at 48W/m: beyond that, heat kills the LED faster than the warranty period. Here's the math.

01

The thermal path matters more than the wattage

Heat has to get from the LED junction, through the PCB, through the extrusion, and out to the air. A 48W fixture with a proper thermal path will outlast a 60W fixture with a poor one. We design for 85°C junction max — not 105°C "absolute maximum."

02

Extrusion profile = the heatsink

The aluminum extrusion isn't just housing — it IS the heatsink. That's why our 30x50 profile is wider than most competitors: more surface area = better heat dissipation = longer life. A skinny profile at high wattage = early failure.

03

LM-80 tested, real-world verified

LEDs are LM-80 tested at 85°C for 5,000 hours in an accredited third-party laboratory. But we also run our own 6,000-hour thermal tests on assembled fixtures to verify real-world performance. The spec sheet says L70 at 50,000h — we make sure the whole fixture actually gets there.

Bottom line: We could quote higher wattages. They'd sell on paper. But we'd be replacing drivers in year three and you'd hate us. 48W/m is the sweet spot for long warranty coverage in a Gulf climate. Need more output? Go wider — the 50x75 profile gives you even better thermal headroom at high wattage.
