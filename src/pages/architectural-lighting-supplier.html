Quality Control

Our 5-stage quality control system.

Quality isn't inspected at the end — it's built in at every stage. We oversee every order with a structured 5-stage QC process, with photos and reports at each checkpoint. You know exactly what you're getting, before it ships.

01

Incoming Material Inspection

Every batch of LEDs, drivers and raw materials is checked on arrival against the BOM — LED binning, driver specification and aluminium alloy composition. Material that does not meet spec is rejected before production.

02

In-Process Monitoring

Checks at key production stages — after machining, after surface treatment, after PCB assembly — cover dimensions, finish quality, solder joints and component placement. Issues are caught early, with photos and reports at each checkpoint.

03

Pre-Assembly Testing

Before final assembly, sub-assemblies are electrically tested for LED current, thermal coupling, driver output and dimming function.

04

Final Review & Inspection

Pre-shipment samples are reviewed against the approved submittal — photometric output, colour consistency, thermal performance and housing integrity — with finish, fit and cosmetic defects inspected visually.

05

Packaging & Shipping Review

Final check of packaging, labeling, and documentation. Cartons are checked for correct labelling, included accessories and complete certification documents. For GCC shipments, customs paperwork is completed before release.

Saudi Arabia

Full SASO 2902 energy efficiency registration via the SABER platform. Product Conformity Certificates (PCoC) for all standard lines, plus Shipment Conformity Certificates (SCoC) for every Saudi-bound order. No customs delays at Jeddah, Dammam, or Riyadh dry port.

MOIAT / ECAS

United Arab Emirates

MOIAT (formerly ESMA)-approved products with ECAS registration for UAE projects. Full compliance with Dubai Municipality and Abu Dhabi Department of Municipalities and Transport requirements. Documentation ready for Jebel Ali and Port of Khalifa customs clearance.

G-Mark

G-Mark certification provides access to all six GCC member states under a single conformity mark. Recognized in Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, and Bahrain. One certificate, six markets.

CE, CB & RoHS
