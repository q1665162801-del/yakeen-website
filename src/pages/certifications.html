Certifications & Compliance

Certified for GCC markets.

SASO 2902 and G-Mark certification is held at the factory level for the product families we supply, with national registration arranged per order for each destination country. Every order ships with the documentation required for customs clearance.

What "certified" means

A certification body has tested the products and issued a certificate (SASO, G-Mark, CE, CB, RoHS). Photometric and performance testing follows industry-standard methods, documented on our Testing & Quality page. Certificates and test reports are supplied with the project submittal pack.
