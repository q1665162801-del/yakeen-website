What "certified" means — and what it doesn't

On this page, "certified" means a third-party certification body has tested our products and issued a certificate (SASO, G-Mark, CE, CB, RoHS). For photometric and performance testing, we work with accredited third-party and partner laboratories following industry-standard methods — documented on our Testing & Quality page.

By Country

Certifications for each GCC market.

Every GCC country has its own certification framework and customs entry requirements. Our standard product lines hold third-party certifications for all six markets, and we provide the exact documentation needed for each destination.

Saudi Arabia

SASO 2902 / SABER

Certification Body

Saudi Standards, Metrology and Quality Organization (SASO), via the SABER electronic platform.

What's Required

  • SASO 2902 energy efficiency registration for all LED lighting products
  • Product Conformity Certificate (PCoC) issued via SABER
  • Shipment Conformity Certificate (SCoC) for each shipment
  • IEC 60598 test reports from accredited labs
  • Product registration on SABER platform

Yakeen Support

Our standard product lines are SASO 2902 registered with valid PCoC certificates. We issue SCoC certificates for every Saudi-bound shipment and provide SABER registration support for smooth customs clearance. Project-specific and custom products are certified per order.

🇦🇪

United Arab Emirates

MOIAT / ECAS

Certification Body

Ministry of Industry and Advanced Technology (MOIAT, formerly ESMA), through the Emirates Conformity Assessment Scheme (ECAS).

What's Required

  • MOIAT product registration (ECAS certificate)
  • Energy efficiency registration for LED lamps and luminaires
  • Valid test reports from MOIAT-recognized laboratories
  • Product marking with MOIAT registration number
  • Party of Record must be registered in UAE

Yakeen Support

For UAE-bound projects, we arrange MOIAT registration under ECAS per order, including energy efficiency certification where required. We provide full ECAS documentation, test reports, and registration details with every UAE shipment to ensure smooth clearance at Jebel Ali, Dubai, and Abu Dhabi ports.

🇶🇦

Qatar

G-Mark

Certification Body

Ministry of Commerce and Industry (MoCI), Qatar. G-Mark (GCC Conformity Mark) is the accepted conformity route.

What's Required

  • Qatar conformity arranged per order (MoCI import requirements)
  • G-Mark certification (GCC-wide recognition)
  • IEC 60598 test reports
  • Commercial invoice with full product details
  • Ministry of Commerce and Industry registration

Yakeen Support

Our standard product lines hold G-Mark certification, and we arrange Qatar conformity per order. We provide complete documentation sets including test reports, certificates, and commercial paperwork for hassle-free clearance at Hamad Port and Doha International Airport.

🇰🇼

Kuwait

KUCAS

Certification Body

Kuwait Institute for Scientific Research (KISR) / Public Authority for Industry (PAI), under the Kuwait Conformity Assurance Scheme (KUCAS).

What's Required

  • KUCAS Technical Registration (TR) for the product
  • Shipment inspection certificate (TIR)
  • IEC 60598 test reports from accredited labs
  • PAI / KISR approved test reports
  • Commercial invoice and packing list

Yakeen Support

For Kuwait-bound projects, we arrange KUCAS Technical Registration per order and work with approved inspection bodies to issue TIR certificates for each shipment, providing complete documentation for smooth clearance at Shuwaikh Port and Kuwait International Airport.

Directorate General for Specifications and Metrology (DGSM), Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion. G-Mark accepted.

What's Required

  • DGSM conformity assessment and registration
  • G-Mark certification (GCC-wide recognition)
  • IEC 60598 test reports
  • Certificate of Origin
  • Complete commercial documentation

We provide full test reports, certificates of conformity, and all commercial documentation required for customs clearance at Port Sultan Qaboos, Salalah Port, and Muscat International Airport.

G-Mark — One Mark, Six Countries

The GCC Conformity Mark (G-Mark) is the Gulf-wide quality and safety mark accepted across all six GCC member states. Products bearing the G-Mark can circulate freely throughout the GCC market without additional national certification requirements. Yakeen Lighting standard product lines carry G-Mark certification; project-specific certifications are arranged per order.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are your products already certified for my country?

Yes — our standard product lines carry SASO 2902 (Saudi) and G-Mark (GCC-wide) certification, providing market access across all six GCC countries. Where a destination additionally requires national registration — MOIAT ECAS (UAE), KUCAS (Kuwait) — we arrange it per order as part of the project. Custom products may require additional certification which we handle as part of the project.

How long does certification take for custom products?

For custom or project-specific products, certification typically takes 4-8 weeks depending on the destination country and product complexity. We start the certification process in parallel with product development to minimize delays. We always recommend factoring certification time into your project schedule, especially for new product types.

What's the difference between SASO, SABER, and SCoC?

SASO is the Saudi Standards Organization that sets the standards. SABER is the electronic platform (Saudi Product Safety Program) where certificates are issued and managed. PCoC (Product Conformity Certificate) is the product-level certificate that's valid for 1 year. SCoC (Shipment Conformity Certificate) is issued per shipment and is what customs actually checks at the border. We handle all of these for you.

Do I need to be registered with customs in each country?

Yes — the party bringing goods into the country must be registered with customs authorities in the destination market. This is a requirement of the customs authorities, not the certification bodies. We can advise on what's needed, but your local customs agent or freight forwarder handles the registration process. We provide all the product-side documentation needed for clearance.

What is G-Mark and does it replace national certifications?

The GCC Conformity Mark (G-Mark) is a Gulf-wide quality mark indicating that a product meets GCC standard requirements. Products with G-Mark can circulate across all six GCC member states. However, some countries (notably Saudi Arabia and UAE) also require their own national registration (SASO SABER and MOIAT ECAS respectively). We hold both G-Mark and individual national registrations where needed.

What if customs has questions about my shipment?

We provide full support through the customs clearance process. If customs authorities have questions or require additional documentation, we respond promptly with whatever is needed. In the rare event of a certification issue, we work directly with the relevant certification body to resolve it. Our goal is zero customs delays for your project.

Do you provide energy efficiency certificates?

Yes — energy efficiency registration is a key part of GCC lighting regulations. Our standard LED product lines are registered under SASO 2902 EER for Saudi Arabia, and we arrange MOIAT energy efficiency registration for the UAE and equivalent schemes in other GCC countries per order. We provide the energy efficiency labels and registration numbers as part of our documentation package.

Can you support Saudi Building Code (SBC) requirements?

Yes — we're familiar with Saudi Energy Efficiency Center (SEEC) requirements and Saudi Building Code (SBC) provisions related to lighting. Our products meet the minimum efficacy requirements and we can provide the documentation needed for project approval and municipal inspection. We also support daylight harvesting and occupancy sensing controls to help meet energy code requirements.
