The Italian Optical Premium

Why specifiers look for Reggiani alternatives.

Reggiani is a venerable Italian lighting house, founded in 1957. Their optical engineering, die-cast aluminum housings, and fixture aesthetics have made them a default specifier choice for luxury hospitality, flagship retail, and high-end commercial projects around the world. But across the GCC — Riyadh, Dubai, Jeddah, Doha — there are practical reasons specifiers look for a smart alternative.

Four reasons specifiers choose Yakeen over Reggiani

1. Value on large BOMs. Reggiani's pricing reflects their 65+ years of R&D, European manufacturing overhead, and global marketing. For a 5-star hotel in Riyadh or a flagship retail rollout across the UAE, that premium compounds across 2,000+ fixtures. We deliver comparable specification-class quality — tier-1 LEDs, precision optics, die-cast aluminum housings — direct from production.

2. Faster delivery to the GCC. Reggiani ships from Italy. Lead times of 10-16 weeks for custom finishes and modified configurations are standard. For fast-track hospitality projects or retail opening dates across Saudi Arabia and the Emirates, that timeline is often a blocker. We typically deliver 3-6 weeks standard, direct to Jebel Ali, Jeddah, or Dammam.

3. Project-specific customization, not a catalog footnote. Reggiani's standard catalog is deep, but project-specific requests — a custom RAL finish, a modified cutout, a non-standard beam angle — get routed through a custom department in Italy with extended lead times and premium pricing. Bespoke modifications are our standard workflow, not an exception.

4. GCC certification as standard. Reggiani ships with CE and CB. SASO 2902, G-Mark, and MOIAT approvals for Saudi and UAE projects typically require an additional certification step, external agents, and 4-6 weeks of lead time. We provide full GCC certification with every order — SASO, G-Mark, MOIAT/ECAS. No customs hold-ups, no surprise fees, no delays at Dubai Municipality or SASO.
