The Designer Label Premium

Why specifiers look for Flos alternatives.

Flos is more than a lighting brand — it's a design icon. Collaborations with Philippe Starck, Achille Castiglioni, Antonio Citterio, and Ronan & Erwan Bouroullec have made Flos the go-to brand for design-forward interiors. But for luxury hospitality, high-end retail, and signature residential projects across the GCC, the price tag and lead times can be prohibitive.

Where Your Flos Budget Goes
Italian designer lighting cost breakdown

With a designer Italian brand, a large share of the budget goes to designer royalties and brand positioning, flagship stores and showrooms, multi-layer distribution margins, and corporate overhead — not the product itself. Yakeen's direct model removes those layers, so a far larger share of your budget goes into the actual product and materials.

Four reasons specifiers choose Yakeen over Flos

1. Design quality without designer pricing. Flos's pricing reflects their A-list designer collaborations, museum-grade brand positioning, and flagship stores in Milan, Paris, and New York. For projects that need beautiful, design-forward lighting but don't require the specific designer name on the spec, Yakeen delivers the same aesthetic class without the designer-brand premium.

2. Customization without the wait. Flos's standard catalog is iconic, but custom modifications — different sizes, custom finishes, project-specific designs — come with significant cost and 12+ week lead times. We specialize in bespoke decorative and architectural lighting. Custom designs that match your design vision, with faster turnaround and streamlined engineering.

3. Faster delivery to the GCC. Flos typically ships from Italy in 10-14 weeks — longer for decorative pieces and custom items. For luxury hotel openings in Dubai, retail fit-outs in Riyadh's Kingdom Centre, or residential projects on the Palm Jumeirah, those timelines can be impossible. Yakeen typically delivers standard products in 3-6 weeks and custom pieces in 6-8 weeks, confirmed at quotation.

4. GCC certification as standard. Flos products come with CE and CB certification, but SASO 2902, G-Mark, and MOIAT/ECAS approvals for Gulf markets often require importers to handle certification themselves — adding cost, time, and risk. We provide full GCC certification as standard. No customs surprises, no additional fees, no paperwork headaches for your Al-Mamlaka or UAE project.
