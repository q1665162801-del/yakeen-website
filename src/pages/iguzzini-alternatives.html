The Italian Design Premium

Why specifiers look for iGuzzini alternatives.

iGuzzini is one of the most respected names in architectural lighting. Their Italian design heritage, optical innovation, and commitment to quality have made them a specifier favorite worldwide. But for projects in Dubai, Riyadh, and across the GCC, there are compelling reasons to consider a smart alternative.

Four reasons specifiers choose Yakeen over iGuzzini

1. Value without compromising design. iGuzzini's pricing reflects their brand heritage, extensive R&D, and global market presence. For large hospitality, retail, and commercial projects across the UAE and KSA, the budget impact adds up quickly. Yakeen delivers comparable specification class — tier-1 Cree/Lumileds LED sources, precision optics, and design-forward form language — direct from production.

2. Faster delivery to the Middle East. iGuzzini typically ships from Italy in 8-12 weeks. For fast-track projects across the GCC, that timeline can be a dealbreaker. Yakeen typically delivers standard products in 3-6 weeks with direct shipping to Jebel Ali, Jeddah, and Dammam.

3. Custom design flexibility. iGuzzini has a beautiful standard catalog, but when you need something specific to your project — a custom finish, a modified form factor, a project-specific optical array — the cost and lead time can grow significantly. We specialize in bespoke architectural lighting. Custom modifications are part of our standard workflow, not an expensive exception.

4. GCC certification as standard. iGuzzini products come with CE and CB certification, but SASO 2902 and G-Mark approvals for Saudi and GCC markets often require additional steps and costs. We provide full GCC certification as standard — SASO, G-Mark, MOIAT/ECAS. No surprises at customs, no additional fees, no delays getting your project approved by Dubai Municipality or Saudi SASO.
