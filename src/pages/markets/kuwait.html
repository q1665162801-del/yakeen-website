FAQ

Kuwait lighting — answered.

Common questions about specifying and sourcing architectural lighting in Kuwait. Can't find what you're looking for? Email us directly.

What certification is required for lighting imported into Kuwait?

Lighting products entering Kuwait require KUCAS (Kuwait Conformity Assurance Scheme) certification and GCC G-Mark compliance. We provide complete KUCAS documentation, including test reports and conformity certificates, for all Kuwait-bound orders.

Which areas of Kuwait do you serve?

We supply projects across all six governorates: Kuwait City, Hawalli, Farwaniya, Ahmadi, Al Jahra, and Mubarak Al-Kabeer. Every order includes complete certification documentation for smooth Kuwait customs clearance.

How quickly can I get a specification review?

You receive a detailed specification review from our engineering team after sending your project details. Typical lead times are 3-4 weeks for standard products and 5-6 weeks for custom fixtures, confirmed at quotation. Rush options are available for urgent projects.

Are your lights suitable for Kuwait's extreme heat?

Yes — all our GCC-spec products are rated for Ta 55°C ambient temperatures, well above Kuwait's summer highs. We use premium thermal management and high-temperature-rated components to ensure reliable performance year-round.

Do you provide custom lighting for Kuwait retail and hospitality projects?

Absolutely — we specialize in custom lighting solutions for retail, hospitality, and commercial projects across Kuwait. From custom track systems for large-scale malls to decorative downlights for boutique hotels, we design and source to your exact specifications.
