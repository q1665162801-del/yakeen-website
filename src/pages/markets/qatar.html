FAQ

Qatar lighting — answered.

Common questions about specifying and sourcing architectural lighting in Qatar. Can't find what you're looking for? Email us directly.

What certifications do lighting products need in Qatar?

Lighting products sold in Qatar must have GCC G-Mark certification and comply with Qatar MoCI (Ministry of Commerce and Industry) regulations. We provide complete G-Mark certification and all documentation needed for Qatar customs clearance and market approval.

Do you have experience with Lusail City projects?

We engineer fixtures suited to commercial and hospitality projects of the type built in Lusail — retail spaces, offices, and mixed-use developments. We understand Lusail City's strict quality and sustainability standards and can provide the required documentation.

What is the delivery timeline to Doha?

Standard products typically ship in 3-4 weeks, custom products in 5-6 weeks; lead times are confirmed at quotation. We provide complete documentation for smooth Qatar customs clearance. Specification review by our engineering team on receipt of your project details.

Can you handle high humidity and coastal conditions in Qatar?

Absolutely. Our exterior fixtures are rated IP66 with marine-grade aluminum and corrosion-resistant finishes — built for Qatar's coastal humidity and heat. All products are rated for Ta 55°C ambient temperatures.

Do you provide photometric reports for Qatar project approvals?

Yes — we provide complete IES photometric files, DIALux calculations, and all technical documentation required for project approvals in Qatar. We can also assist with energy efficiency calculations for green building requirements.
