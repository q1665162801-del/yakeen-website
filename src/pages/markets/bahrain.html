FAQ

Bahrain lighting — answered.

Common questions about specifying and sourcing architectural lighting in Bahrain. Can't find what you're looking for? Email us directly.

What certifications are needed for lighting products in Bahrain?

Lighting products in Bahrain require GCC G-Mark certification and must comply with Bahrain Ministry of Industry and Commerce (MOIC) regulations. We provide complete G-Mark certification and all documentation needed for Bahrain customs clearance.

Which areas of Bahrain do you serve?

We supply projects across all of Bahrain including Manama, Riffa, Muharraq, Isa Town, Sitra, Hamad Town, and Budaiya. From the Bahrain Bay developments to Seef district retail, every order includes complete certification documentation.

How long does delivery take to Bahrain?

Standard products typically ship in 3-4 weeks and custom fixtures in 5-6 weeks; lead times are confirmed at quotation. You'll get a detailed specification review from our engineering team. We provide all G-Mark documentation for smooth Bahrain customs clearance.

Are your lights suitable for Bahrain's island climate?

Yes — Bahrain's island humidity and salty air are no problem. Our exterior fixtures are IP66 rated with marine-grade aluminum and corrosion-resistant finishes. All products are rated for Ta 55°C with UV-stabilized materials.

Do you work on Bahrain's commercial and hospitality projects?

Absolutely. Typical applications we engineer for include retail developments in Seef, hospitality projects in Juffair, and office buildings in Bahrain Bay. Our custom capabilities and fast turnaround make us ideal for Bahrain's fast-moving commercial sector.
