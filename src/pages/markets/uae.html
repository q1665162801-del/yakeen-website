FAQ

UAE lighting — answered.

Common questions about specifying and sourcing architectural lighting in the United Arab Emirates. Can't find what you're looking for? Email us directly.

What certifications are required for lighting in the UAE?

Lighting products imported into the UAE require MOIAT (Ministry of Industry and Advanced Technology, formerly ESMA) certification and must comply with ECAS (Emirates Conformity Assessment Scheme). We provide complete MOIAT certification documentation for all UAE-bound orders, including test reports and conformity certificates required by Dubai Municipality and Abu Dhabi DMT.

Can you deliver to projects across all seven emirates?

Yes — we regularly supply projects in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah, Fujairah, and Umm Al Quwain. Every order includes complete MOIAT documentation for smooth customs clearance anywhere in the UAE.

What is the typical lead time for projects in Dubai?

Standard products typically ship in 3-4 weeks; custom fixtures take 5-6 weeks; lead times are confirmed at quotation. Specification review by our engineering team. For fast-track projects in Dubai, ask about our expedited production option.

Do you support Dubai Municipality and Abu Dhabi DMT requirements?

We prepare the documentation a municipal submission needs — photometric data for the configuration specified, energy calculations and compliance documents. What applies to your project is confirmed against the product selection before you commit.

Are your products suitable for UAE coastal environments?

Yes — our exterior fixtures are available with marine-grade 316 stainless steel hardware and salt-spray tested finishes, making them ideal for coastal projects in Dubai, Abu Dhabi corniche, and the Northern Emirates.
