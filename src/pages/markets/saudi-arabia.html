Quick Answers

Common questions about lighting in Saudi Arabia.

Short, direct answers to the questions we get most often from specifiers, contractors, and project managers working in KSA.

Is Yakeen Lighting certified for Saudi Arabia?

Yes. All Yakeen standard product lines are SASO 2902 energy efficiency registered with valid Product Conformity Certificates (PCoC) issued via the SABER platform. We also hold GCC G-Mark certification. We issue Shipment Conformity Certificates (SCoC) for every Saudi-bound shipment.

What lighting certifications are required in Saudi Arabia?

LED lighting products require SASO 2902 energy efficiency registration (PCoC) through the SABER platform, plus a Shipment Conformity Certificate (SCoC) for each shipment. Products must meet IEC 60598 safety standards. G-Mark certification provides additional GCC-wide market access.

How long does SABER certification take?

For pre-registered products (which all Yakeen standard lines are), the SCoC shipment certificate is typically issued within 2-3 business days. For new or custom products requiring initial PCoC registration, plan for 4-6 weeks including testing. We start the process in parallel with production where possible.

What's the warranty on lighting fixtures in Saudi Arabia?

Warranty is specified per project — typically 5 years, extendable to 8 depending on product family, drivers, and operating environment — covering LED sources, drivers, housings, and finish. Every fixture is tested before shipping, and we stand behind our quality.

Can lighting fixtures handle the heat in Saudi Arabia?

Yes, but you need to specify correctly. Summer ambient temperatures in Saudi Arabia can reach 45-50°C. For exterior fixtures, we specify drivers and components rated for Ta 55°C or higher. For interior fixtures in unconditioned spaces, thermal derating is important. All our fixtures are tested at elevated temperatures in our thermal chamber.

Does Yakeen have a local presence in Saudi Arabia?

We work remotely with clients across Saudi Arabia — Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam, Mecca, Medina, and the NEOM development zone. All SASO/SABER documentation is handled in advance, and we coordinate closely with your project team throughout installation and commissioning.
