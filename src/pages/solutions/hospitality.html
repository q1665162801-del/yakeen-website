The guest journey

From arrival to turn-down. One lighting system, many scenes.

Hospitality lighting isn't just about how a space looks at 7pm. It's about the right light at 6am breakfast, 2pm check-in, and midnight corridor walks. Here's how we think about it.

18:00

Arrival & Lobby

Warm, dimmed ambient light signals "you've arrived." Accent lighting on art and features sets the tone. Correlated color temperature around 2700K — never cool white in a hotel lobby.

20:00

Restaurant & Bar

Lower ambient, higher contrast on tables. People eat with their eyes first — CRI 95+ is the minimum, 98 available for premium applications. Dimmed pathways keep the intimate feel while maintaining safety.

22:00

Guest Room & Corridors

Corridors dim to night mode — enough light to find your room, not enough to wake you up. Guest rooms have multiple scenes: reading, relaxing, working, and a proper night setting that kills every standby light.

07:00

Breakfast & Morning

Brighter, slightly cooler light (3000K) for the breakfast area. People need to see their food and read the paper. Corridors and public areas ramp up smoothly — no sudden "flick on" at 6am.
