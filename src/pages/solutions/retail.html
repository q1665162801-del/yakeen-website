Why it matters

Good retail lighting pays for itself.

Lighting is the most cost-effective lever you have for changing how people behave in a store. Get it right and they stay longer, buy more, and come back. Get it wrong and they walk out.

Longer

Dwell time

Well-lit stores with good vertical illumination make people slow down and browse. They don't just walk through — they look.

CRI 95+

Color accuracy

Products look the way they're supposed to. Red is red, blue is blue, skin tones look healthy. Fewer returns, more confidence at the register.

2:1

Accent ratio

Twice as bright on the merchandise as on the ceiling. The eye goes where the light is — make sure it's going to your products, not the HVAC.

We don't have a magic formula — every brand is different. But we know the levers. Tell us what you're selling and we'll tell you how to light it.
