How It Works

Simple process.
No surprises.

We're a focused team, which means things move fast. You talk directly to the engineers handling your project — no sales department, no handoffs.

Send us your drawings

BOQ, CAD files, lighting layout — whatever you have. Even a sketch works. We'll review it and come back to you.

Not sure? Order 1–3 samples. Check the finish, test the light quality, show your client. Only then decide on the full order.

We send you photos and a simple QC report before your order ships. If something's wrong, we fix it first.
