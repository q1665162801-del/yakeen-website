The German Outdoor Standard

Why specifiers look for BEGA alternatives.

BEGA is the gold standard for architectural outdoor lighting. German-engineered, built like a tank, and designed to last for decades in harsh environments. But for facade, landscape, and exterior projects across the GCC, BEGA's cost and lead times often push specifiers to explore alternatives that deliver comparable specification-grade build quality.

Four reasons specifiers choose Yakeen over BEGA

1. Competitive value through our vetted supply chain. BEGA's pricing reflects their German manufacturing, premium materials, and brand reputation. For large-scale exterior projects — building facades, public realm, landscape lighting across Saudi Arabia and the UAE — the budget impact is significant. Yakeen delivers specification-grade build quality and outdoor performance at exceptional value, through our specialized production network.

2. Shorter lead times. BEGA typically ships from Germany in 8-12 weeks. For fast-track facade and public-realm projects across the GCC, that's often too slow. Yakeen typically delivers standard outdoor fixtures in 4-6 weeks, with expedited options available; exact lead times are confirmed at quotation.

3. Built for Gulf conditions. BEGA designs for European climates. We design for the Arabian Gulf. Higher ambient temperature ratings (+55°C), UV-stable finishes that don't chalk in the desert sun, corrosion resistance for coastal salt spray — our outdoor fixtures are engineered specifically for the conditions in Al-Mamlaka and the Emirates.

4. GCC certification as standard. BEGA holds CE, CB, and sometimes UL certification. But SASO 2902, G-Mark, and MOIAT/ECAS approvals for the Gulf market are not part of their standard offering. We provide full GCC certification as standard on every outdoor fixture, with all documentation ready for Saudi and UAE authorities.

Built to Last Outdoors

Comparable materials. Specification-grade build.

BEGA's reputation is built on over-the-top build quality — thick aluminum housings, tempered glass, marine-grade corrosion protection. We use comparable specification-grade materials and construction methods, because outdoor lighting in the Gulf needs to be tough.

Housings

Die-Cast & Extruded Aluminum

High-grade die-cast aluminum and 6063 extruded aluminum — the material standard for specification-grade outdoor luminaires. Thick wall sections for structural rigidity and thermal mass. Precision CNC machined mating surfaces for perfect sealing and alignment.

Corrosion Protection

Anodized + Architectural Powder Coat

Multi-layer corrosion protection: chromate conversion pre-treatment, anodized base layer, and architectural-grade polyester powder coat top finish. Marine-grade (C5-M) coatings available for coastal projects in Jeddah, Dammam, and the UAE.

Glass Optics

Tempered Borosilicate Glass

Tempered borosilicate glass lenses — a specification-grade material chosen for high light transmission, excellent thermal shock resistance, and impact resistance well above standard soda-lime glass. Anti-reflective coating option available.

Sealing System

Silicone Gaskets · IP66 / IP67 Rated

Continuous compression-molded silicone gaskets for reliable long-term sealing. Our IP66 and IP67 rated fixtures withstand dust storms, heavy rain, and pressure washing — essential for maintaining performance in the harsh Gulf environment.

Thermal Management

Passive Cooling · +55°C Rated

Fin-style heat sinks optimized for natural convection. Designed for continuous operation at 55°C ambient temperature — because summer in Riyadh and Dubai doesn't follow European temperature standards.

LED Engines

Cree · Lumileds · Nichia

Same tier-1 LED sources: Cree XLamp and Lumileds LUXEON families as standard, Nichia on request. High-efficacy COB arrays for floodlights and projectors, mid-power SMD for linear and grazing applications. 3-step SDCM binning standard.

Where BEGA excels — and where we match them.

BEGA is a legendary German outdoor lighting brand with a reputation for bulletproof build quality. We respect that. Here's an honest look at where BEGA leads, and where Yakeen matches or exceeds them for exterior and facade projects in the GCC.

BEGA Strength

Over-the-Top Build Quality

BEGA's fixtures are famously overbuilt. Thick aluminum castings, heavy-duty hardware, and a no-compromise approach to structural integrity. Their fixtures can survive just about anything — and they look like they could survive a direct asteroid impact.

Yakeen position: Our build quality meets all the same international standards (IP67, IK08/IK10, project-based warranty). We use comparable specification-grade materials and adequate wall thicknesses. The difference is that Bega's engineering targets specifications beyond what many projects require. We build to the spec your project needs.

BEGA Strength

Heritage Product Range

BEGA has been making outdoor lighting since 1948. Their catalog is enormous, with hundreds of fixture families spanning every conceivable outdoor application — from bollards to floodlights to heritage-style lanterns. The depth of their standard range is unmatched.

Yakeen position: Our standard outdoor catalog covers the fixture types used in the vast majority of projects. For anything outside our standard range, we offer direct custom development through our engineering team.
