Photometric Data & Calculations

Does Yakeen provide IES photometric files?
Yes. Our main product lines carry LM-63 photometric files measured to LM-79 methodology, issued with the project submittal.

You can use them directly in DIALux, Relux, AGi32, and other lighting calculation software.

IES files are free and available upon request — or you can generate a custom specification with our AI Specification Assistant and get all relevant IES files instantly.
Can Yakeen support DIALux or Relux lighting calculations?
Yes. Our IES files work natively with DIALux evo, DIALux pro, and Relux.

For project support, our lighting design team can provide completed DIALux calculation models using Yakeen fixtures — including falsecolor renderings, illuminance charts, UGR analysis, and uniformity reports.

This is complimentary for projects where Yakeen is specified. Just send us your floor plan and lighting requirements, and we'll return a complete calculation package — timing confirmed with your project brief.
What photometric testing do you perform?
Production batches are photometrically tested to LM-79 methodology before release.

We measure:
• Total luminous flux and efficacy
• Luminous intensity distribution (beam angles and candela values)
• Correlated color temperature (CCT) and chromaticity
• Color rendering index (CRI/Ra, R9, R13)
• Power factor and THD
• Thermal performance and case temperature

Testing follows LM-79 methodology. Test reports are provided with project documentation.

Specification-Grade Lighting 101

What is specification-grade LED lighting?
Specification-grade LED lighting refers to commercial and architectural luminaires engineered to meet precise performance criteria for professional projects. Unlike consumer or commodity lighting, spec-grade fixtures have verified photometric data, controlled color consistency (MacAdam 3-step or better), documented driver performance, thermal management validated by LM-80 testing, and published efficiency ratings. They are intended for use by architects, lighting designers, and MEP engineers who need to write them into construction specifications with confidence that the delivered product will match the documented performance.
What is the difference between commercial grade and specification grade lighting?
Commercial-grade lighting is built for basic commercial use with acceptable performance and reliability. Specification-grade lighting is a higher tier designed for architectural specification — it includes full photometric testing reports (IES files), tighter color tolerances, better optical control (UGR ratings, precise beam angles), longer warranties (5+ years), premium drivers from brands like Philips Xitanium or Tridonic, and complete submittal documentation. Spec-grade fixtures can be written into project specifications with performance guarantees, while commercial-grade is typically selected by contractors based on price and basic fit.
What does CRI 90+ mean and when do I need it?
CRI (Color Rendering Index) measures how accurately a light source renders colors compared to a reference source. CRI 90+ means the light source scores 90 or higher on the 0-100 CRI scale, indicating excellent color rendering. You need CRI 90+ in spaces where color accuracy matters: retail (especially fashion and food), hospitality (lobbies, restaurants, guest rooms), high-end residential, galleries, and healthcare facilities. For premium projects, CRI 98 with R9>50 is recommended for true museum-grade color quality. Yakeen fixtures are available in CRI 90+ standard and CRI 98 premium options.
What is UGR<19 and why is it important for office lighting?
UGR (Unified Glare Rating) is a standardized measure of discomfort glare from luminaires in an interior space. UGR<19 means the fixture produces low enough glare to be suitable for general office environments where people work at screens and on documents. Achieving UGR<19 requires deep-recessed optics, louvers, or specific lens designs — it cannot be achieved by standard flat-panel or basic downlight fixtures. For open-plan offices, meeting rooms, and educational spaces, UGR<19 is typically specified to ensure visual comfort and productivity.
What are IES photometric files and why do I need them?
IES photometric files are standardized data files that contain the complete light distribution characteristics of a luminaire — intensity values at every vertical and horizontal angle, measured in candelas. Lighting designers use these files in software like DIALux, Relux, and AGi32 to calculate light levels, uniformity, glare ratings, and energy consumption for a project. Without IES files, a designer cannot accurately simulate how a fixture will perform in a space, which means the fixture cannot be properly specified. Yakeen main product lines come with IES LM-63 format photometric files measured to LM-79 methodology.

GCC Compliance & Certification

What is SASO 2902 certification for LED lighting?
SASO 2902 is the Saudi Standards, Metrology and Quality Organization's energy efficiency certification program for lighting products sold in Saudi Arabia. It replaced the original SASO certification scheme and sets minimum efficacy (lumens per watt) requirements for LED lamps and luminaires. Products must be tested by an accredited laboratory and registered through the SABER platform to obtain a Certificate of Conformity (CoC) before they can be imported into Saudi Arabia. SASO 2902 covers both energy performance and safety requirements based on IEC 60598-1 standards.
What is G-Mark certification and do I need it for GCC projects?
G-Mark (Gulf Conformity Mark) is a regional certification mark indicating that a product meets the technical requirements of Gulf Standardization Organization (GSO) standards. It is required for certain product categories being sold in GCC countries. For LED lighting products, G-Mark certification demonstrates compliance with Gulf standards for safety and performance. While not all GCC markets require G-Mark for every lighting product, having G-Mark certification simplifies market access across all six GCC countries (Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, Bahrain) and is increasingly specified in large-scale government and commercial tenders.
What lighting certifications are required for Saudi Arabia projects?
For LED lighting products in Saudi Arabia, the primary requirements are:

SASO 2902 Energy Efficiency Certification — mandatory for most LED lighting products, registered through SABER.
SABER Product Registration — online platform for obtaining Certificates of Conformity for customs clearance.
IEC 60598-1 Safety — luminaire safety standard, required as the basis for SASO certification.
G-Mark (optional but recommended) — Gulf-wide conformity mark, often required for government and large-scale tenders.

Yakeen Lighting provides full SASO 2902 certified product lines with complete SABER registration support and documentation for Saudi projects.
What is SABER and how does it work for lighting imports?
SABER (Saudi Electronic System for Conformity Assessment and Clearance) is the Saudi Standards, Metrology and Quality Organization's (SASO) online platform for managing product conformity assessment and issuing Certificates of Conformity for imported goods. For lighting products, the process works as follows: (1) The registering party or provider registers the product on SABER, (2) a SASO 2902 accredited testing lab tests the product and issues a test report, (3) a certification body reviews the documentation and issues a Product Certificate of Conformity (PCOC), and (4) for each shipment, a Shipment Certificate of Conformity (SCOC) is issued. The SCOC is required for customs clearance at Saudi ports.
How long does SASO certification take for LED lighting products?
The typical timeline for SASO 2902 certification for LED lighting products is 3-6 weeks from sample submission to certificate issuance. This includes: 1-2 weeks for laboratory testing (photometric, electrical safety, and EMC tests), 1-2 weeks for documentation review by the certification body, and approximately 1 week for SABER platform registration and certificate issuance. Rush options are sometimes available. Yakeen Lighting maintains active SASO 2902 certifications for our core product lines, so certified fixtures can ship immediately without waiting for new certification.
Do you provide MOIAT certification for the UAE market?
Yes, we provide MOIAT (Ministry of Industry and Advanced Technology, formerly ESMA) compliance support for UAE projects. Our product lines meet ECAS (Emirates Conformity Assessment Scheme) requirements, and we provide all necessary documentation including test reports, technical files, and declaration of conformity for UAE market access. For Dubai-specific projects, we can also provide documentation supporting Dubai Municipality requirements and DEWA specifications.
