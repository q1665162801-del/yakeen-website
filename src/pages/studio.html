Why We Exist

Premium lighting shouldn't be a luxury only the biggest budgets can afford.

We started Yakeen because we saw a gap in the market. The world's best architectural lighting was locked behind European brand names, exorbitant showrooms, and layers of distribution markup. Yet the components, the engineering, and the craftsmanship — those are universally available to those who know where to look.

We build to the same specification class as Europe's leading architectural lighting houses, with the same engineering principles and the same quality-control discipline. Cree and Lumileds LEDs. Mean Well drivers. Precision-machined aircraft-grade aluminum. IES-verified photometrics. The difference? We work direct with the project team — no showrooms, no brand markup, no distributor layers.

We are engineers and designers who genuinely love light. We are not salespeople chasing quotas. Every fixture we deliver comes with full documentation and a project warranty. Every project is a collaboration with architects and designers who expect more from their architectural lighting partner.

Our Craft

01

Optical Engineering

Photometric design with precision beam shaping, controlled glare, and exacting light distribution. Every lens and reflector is engineered for purpose.

02

55°C rated housings with precision heat dissipation paths. Engineered thermal profiles that protect LED longevity and maintain consistent color.

05

Bespoke Fabrication

Custom dimensions, curved profiles, special optics, and unique finishes. If you can draw it, we can engineer and build it to specification.

Sustainability

Responsible by design

Great lighting should last decades, not years. We deliver fixtures built for longevity, efficiency, and circularity — because the most sustainable product is the one you never need to replace.

Energy Efficiency

Main product lines photometrically tested to LM-79 methodology, with high efficacy ratings. Designed to minimize energy consumption without sacrificing quality of light.

Built to Last

Project warranty — typically 5 years, extendable to 8 — and 50,000+ hour L70 lifespan. Serviceable design means components can be replaced rather than entire fixtures.

Recyclable Materials

Aircraft-grade aluminum housings — fully recyclable at end of life. All products RoHS compliant, free from hazardous substances.

Responsible Finishing

Production uses zero-waste finishing with powder coating recovery and responsible waste management.
