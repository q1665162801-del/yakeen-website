Whole-Villa Enquiries

Have a BOQ or a drawing set? Send it as it is.

Most villa enquiries arrive as a spreadsheet and a PDF. You don't need to reformat anything — send what the consultant gave you and we'll mark it up.

A BOQ

Item, description, quantity — even if the descriptions came from someone else. We'll map it to what we actually supply and flag the lines we'd replace.

A lighting layout

PDF, DWG or DIALux output. We'll count the points, check CCT and beam angles against the design intent, and tell you where the quantities look thin.

Ceiling and structural drawings

For decorative pieces we need the slab, the void depth and the finished ceiling level before we can issue a suspension load schedule.

A schedule or a sketch

If nothing exists yet, a room list with rough dimensions is enough to start. We'll build the first scope split with you.

How to send the files. Submit the form with the approximate scope, then send the BOQ or drawings by WhatsApp quoting your project name — large PDFs and DWGs don't survive contact forms.

What you get back. One document: scope split by category, quantities checked against your drawings, a suspension load schedule for the decorative pieces, the certification scope, and one price for the package.
Frequently Asked

Common questions about starting a project.

How fast will I hear back?

Every project gets a full specification review from our engineering team. Complex custom projects may take longer as we prepare technical drawings and calculations. We always confirm receipt promptly.

Do you offer samples?

Yes. Samples carry a refundable deposit, credited toward your production order. Standard samples ship quickly; project and custom sample schedules are confirmed at quotation.

What certifications do you have?

SASO 2902 energy efficiency, GCC G-Mark, CE, RoHS, and IEC 60598 compliance. We handle SABER registration for Saudi Arabia imports.

What's the minimum project size?

There's no strict minimum for specification-grade custom fixtures. We work with projects of all sizes — from boutique fit-outs to large-scale developments. Every project gets the same engineering attention.

How long does a custom project take?

Specification-grade modifications, engineered-to-order fixtures, and fully bespoke designs each run on a different schedule. Lead times are confirmed at quotation; ask about expedited production for time-sensitive projects.

Do you ship to all GCC countries?

Yes — Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, and Bahrain. We handle customs documentation and can arrange door-to-door delivery.
