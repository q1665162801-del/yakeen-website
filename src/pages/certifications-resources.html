Certifications

Global standards. Locally verified.

Every Yakeen Lighting product is specified and supplied to meet the most rigorous international safety, performance, and environmental standards. Certifications are third-party verified and regularly audited — and the supporting test reports ship with every project.

EU Compliance

CE Marking

All Yakeen Lighting products carry the CE mark, demonstrating compliance with European Union health, safety, and environmental protection requirements.

CE marking is a producer's declaration that a product meets all applicable EU directives and regulations. For lighting products, this covers both electrical safety and electromagnetic compatibility.

Applicable Directives

  • LVD 2014/35/EU — Low Voltage Directive: electrical safety for products operating between 50–1000V AC
  • EMC 2014/30/EU — Electromagnetic Compatibility: emissions and immunity requirements
  • RoHS 2011/65/EU — Restriction of Hazardous Substances (see separate card)
  • ERP (EU) 2019/2020 — Energy-Related Products: eco-design requirements
Environmental

RoHS 3

Our products comply with the EU Restriction of Hazardous Substances Directive (RoHS 3 / 2015/863/EU), limiting the use of ten hazardous substances in electrical and electronic equipment.

RoHS compliance is not just about meeting regulations — it reflects our commitment to environmental responsibility and the long-term sustainability of our products and their production.

Restricted Substances

  • Lead (Pb), Mercury (Hg), Cadmium (Cd)
  • Hexavalent chromium (Cr VI)
  • Polybrominated biphenyls (PBB)
  • Polybrominated diphenyl ethers (PBDE)
  • DEHP, BBP, DBP, DIBP (phthalates)
International

CB Scheme (IECEE)

Our GCC certifications are built on CB test reports from IECEE-accredited laboratories — the international system for mutual acceptance of product safety test results.

Testing by an IECEE-accredited body means results are recognized by all 54 member countries, which streamlines national certification in our target markets.

Key Benefits

  • Recognized in 54 member countries globally
  • Based on IEC international standards
  • Simplifies national certification in target markets
  • Reduces redundant testing and certification costs
  • IEC 60598-1 (luminaires) and IEC 62031 (LED modules)
🇸🇦
Saudi Arabia

SASO IECEE

Our products are recognized under the SASO IECEE scheme, providing direct access to the Saudi Arabian market through the SABER electronic certification platform.

SASO (Saudi Standards, Metrology and Quality Organization) accepts IECEE CB certificates as the basis for product registration in Saudi Arabia. This means our products can be quickly registered on the SABER platform with minimal additional testing.

SABER Registration

  • Product Conformity Certificates (PCoC) issued via SABER
  • Shipment Conformity Certificates (SCoC) per shipment
  • SASO 2902 energy efficiency registration
  • Full documentation support for customs clearance
  • IEC 60598 test reports
Ingress Protection

IP Ratings

Our product range covers multiple Ingress Protection ratings, from interior-only fixtures to fully weatherproof outdoor luminaires. Each rating indicates the level of protection against solid objects and water ingress.

All IP ratings are verified through third-party testing in accordance with IEC 60529. We provide the appropriate IP rating for every application — from lobbies and corridors to building facades and landscape lighting.

Available Ratings

IP20
Finger safe. Indoor dry areas.
IP44
Splash proof. Bathrooms, balconies.
IP54
Dust protected. Sheltered outdoor.
IP65
Dust tight. Jet-proof. General outdoor.
IP66
Dust tight. Powerful jets. Harsh outdoor.
Quality Management

ISO 9001:2015

Production is managed to ISO 9001:2015, the internationally recognized standard for quality management systems. This certification underpins every aspect of the production process.

ISO 9001 means documented procedures for quality planning, control, assurance, and improvement. Regular surveillance audits ensure ongoing compliance and continuous improvement.

Quality Principles

  • Customer focus and continuous improvement
  • Process-based approach to manufacturing
  • Incoming inspection of all components
  • In-process quality control at every stage
  • Final product testing before shipment
  • Documented procedures and traceability
Quality Assurance

Tested. Verified. Documented.

Quality is built into every stage of the order — from component selection to pre-shipment review. Reports from accredited laboratories and documented inspection records ship with every batch.

01

Incoming Component Inspection

Components are inspected before production. Specifications are verified, critical parts are tested by accredited laboratories, and traceability is maintained throughout the supply chain.

  • LED binning verification (Cree, Lumileds, Seoul)
  • Driver functionality testing (Mean Well, Philips)
  • Material dimension and finish checks
  • provider quality audits and scorecards
  • Full traceability by batch and lot number
02

In-Process Quality Control

Quality checks are performed at every critical stage of assembly. Standardised checklists and measurement tools catch issues before they move downstream.

  • CNC machining dimensional verification
  • Surface finish and coating thickness testing
  • Optical alignment and beam pattern checks
  • Thermal interface material application audit
  • Soldering and wiring inspection
03

Electrical & Performance Verification

Electrical and photometric performance is verified per production batch to IEC 60598 and LM-79, with the reports supplied in the submittal pack.

  • Hi-pot (dielectric withstand) verification to IEC 60598
  • Ground continuity and insulation resistance
  • Power consumption and power factor verification
  • Photometric verification against the IES data for each batch
  • Thermal performance checked against the LM-80 / TM-21 projection
04

Pre-Shipment Release

Before shipment, every order is reviewed against the approved submittal, with the document pack checked and released with the goods.

  • Pre-shipment sample reviewed against the approved submittal
  • Visual inspection of finish and assembly quality
  • Accessory and documentation completeness check
  • Packaging review for transport protection
  • Written release sign-off before shipment
