What We Engineer

Engineering capabilities for every project challenge.

Nine core engineering capabilities that we bring to every project. Customization is just one of them — the real value is in the engineering that makes it work.

01

Specification Engineering

We help you write precise, buildable specifications that are clear, complete, and actually achievable in production.

02

Optical Engineering

Custom beam angles, lens design, reflector geometry, and glare control. Engineered light distributions for your exact application.

03

Thermal Engineering

Thermal simulation, heat sink design, and LED longevity optimization. Engineered thermal paths that protect performance.

04

Mechanical Engineering

Custom form factors, mounting systems, IP rating, and structural analysis. Precision-machined housings with tight tolerances.

05

Electrical Engineering

Driver matching, dimming protocols, DALI-2 integration, and emergency backup. Full GCC electrical standard compliance.

06

Custom Dimensions

Precision-engineered sizes and proportions for your exact space. No awkward gaps, no visible joints, no compromises.

07

Custom Optics

Project-specific light distributions, not catalog beam angles. We engineer the exact beam your project needs.

08

Custom Finishes

Color matching, texture matching, and material matching to your interior. Architectural-grade finishes specified to your design intent.

09

Photometric Testing

Photometric reports to LM-79, with LM-63 IES file generation and DIALux verification. Performance data you can specify with confidence. See what backs the data →
