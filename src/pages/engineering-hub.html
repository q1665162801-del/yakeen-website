What We Engineer

From optic to installation. Every detail engineered.

Six core engineering disciplines that come together in every fixture we deliver. From the beam of light that leaves the lens to the mounting detail that goes into the ceiling, everything is engineered to specification.

Optical Engineering

Lens design, reflector geometry, beam shaping, glare control, and IES file generation. Every optic is engineered for precise light distribution and UGR control.

Thermal Management

Heat sink design, thermal simulation, LED longevity optimization, and photometric testing to LM-79 methodology. Engineered thermal paths that protect performance and extend fixture life.

Mechanical Engineering

Fixture design, mounting systems, ingress protection, and structural analysis. Precision-machined housings with tight tolerances for seamless architectural integration.

Electrical Engineering

Driver selection, dimming protocols, DALI-2 integration, and emergency lighting. Full compliance with GCC electrical standards and control system compatibility.

Material & Finish

Anodized aluminum, powder coat, custom colors, and corrosion resistance. Architectural-grade finishes specified to match your design intent and environment.

Quality Assurance

Incoming inspection, in-process QC, third-party reports, and certification documentation. Every batch reviewed against the approved submittal before release.

Our Model

Engineer-led. Specification-grade.

We're not a production line. We're an engineering team that runs production through specialist lines across the region. Each line excels at a specific fixture type — downlights, linear systems, track lighting, custom fabrication.

We manage the engineering, quality control, and certification so you get expert-level quality without the brand premium. The result is fixtures that perform to European photometric standards, with a lean cost structure.
