What We Do

Lighting design services.

A complete lighting design service covering every stage of your project, from initial concept through to final commissioning.

◈

Concept Design

Lighting strategy, mood boards, and conceptual layouts that establish the visual direction for your project.

◆

Scheme Design

Detailed lighting layouts, fixture selection, and technical specification developed in coordination with the design team.

◇

Technical Documentation

Construction documentation, specification schedules, wiring diagrams, and control system design.

◐

DIALux & Calculation

Lighting calculations and visualizations using DIALux and Relux to verify light levels, uniformity, and glare control.

⬡

Remote Commissioning Support

Remote engineering guidance for installation, focusing, and dimming curve calibration to ensure the installed lighting performs as designed.

◊

Control System Design

DALI, DMX, and KNX control system design, including scene setting, scheduling, and user interface specification.

Our Process

How we work.

01
